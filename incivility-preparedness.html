Before You Get There: Preparing for Preceptor Incivility

You start clinical in the spring. Most preceptors are generous teachers, and hard feedback is part of learning anesthesia. Some behavior, though, crosses from teaching into incivility. This exercise lets you practice telling the difference and responding, before it happens for real.
