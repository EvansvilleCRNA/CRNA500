Innovation pitch canvas

Work through each section to build your pitch using the value-based outcomes framework from Chapter 5. When you're finished, export your responses into a summary you can submit as your one-page written pitch.

Fill in every section below. There are no word limits — write as much or as little as the idea needs. When you're done, use Export summary at the bottom to generate text you can paste into your submission.

Section 1

Current state / problem

What's inefficient, unsafe, or outdated in current practice? Be specific about the setting and who it affects.

Section 2

Proposed innovation

What's your idea? Describe it clearly enough that someone unfamiliar with the problem could picture it.

Section 3

Stakeholders affected

Who feels this change — patients, care team, institution, others? Name them specifically.

Section 4

Value triad

How does the innovation improve cost, quality, and experience? This is the core of the Chapter 5 value-based outcomes framework — address all three.

Section 5

Anticipated barriers

What could block adoption — cost, culture, workflow, regulation? How would you address each one?
